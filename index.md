Puppy Panic skates for Birmingham Roller Derby’s B Team.

It started roller derby in February 2025 after being convinced to try it by one of its friends who skates for Toronto.

In April 2025 it unfortunately broke its ankle at training, but was quickly back on wheels and had soon passed minimums and was playing games!
